Audiobook Player

A lightweight audiobook player for Windows.

Audiobook Player plays audiobooks from your PC and, optionally, from your own Google Drive. It is a personal project and is not distributed through any store.

  • Groups chapter files into books automatically, in the right order, including multi-disc books.
  • Plays single-file books (m4b, mp3) with embedded chapters.
  • Remembers your place, with bookmarks, speed control and a sleep timer.
  • Optional Google Drive library: stream or download books for offline listening.

Google Drive access is read-only and used only to list and play the audio files in the folders you choose. See the privacy policy and the terms of service.
