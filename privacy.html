Audiobook Player: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Audiobook Player ("the app") is a desktop audiobook player for Windows. It runs entirely on your own computer. The developer operates no servers and receives none of your data.

What the app accesses

  • Audio files on your PC in the folders you add, to build your library and play your books.
  • Google Drive (optional). If you sign in with Google, the app requests read-only access to your Drive (the drive.readonly scope) and your email address (to show which account is signed in). It uses this access only to list the folders you browse, read the audio files and cover images in the folders you add to your Cloud library, and play or download those files.

What is stored, and where

  • Your library, listening progress, bookmarks and settings are stored in a local folder on your PC.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored in that same local folder. Signing out in the app deletes it.
  • Books you choose to download for offline listening are saved to a local cache on your PC. You can remove them at any time in the app.
  • None of this is uploaded to, or shared with, the developer or any third party.

Network requests the app makes

  • Google APIs, only when you use the Cloud library, to list and fetch your files.
  • Cover-art lookup. For books with no cover, the app sends the book's title and author to Apple's public iTunes Search API and to Open Library to find a cover image. Nothing else is sent.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide the features described above. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not read by any person.

Revoking access

You can sign out inside the app, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
